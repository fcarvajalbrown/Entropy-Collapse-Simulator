Dear Editor-in-Chief,

I am pleased to submit my manuscript, "An Entropy Robustness Index for Planar
Steel Frames: Formulation, Verified Implementation, and Comparison with Standard
Collapse Criteria," for consideration as a full original article in the Latin
American Journal of Solids and Structures.

The paper introduces the Entropy Robustness Index R_S, a bounded, dimensionless,
calibration-free measure of structural robustness obtained from the Shannon
entropy of the elastic strain-energy distribution evaluated under the
alternate-load-path (notional member-removal) procedure. Unlike the displacement,
demand-capacity-ratio, and energy criteria, each of which requires a limit
calibrated to the structure at hand, R_S needs no tuned threshold. The work
contributes (i) the index and a provable determinacy bound (R_S = 0 for a
statically determinate structure), (ii) evidence that R_S rises monotonically
with the degree of static indeterminacy and that its member-criticality ranking
is not a re-encoding of compliance-based importance, and (iii) a four-criteria
comparison on a single incremental analysis. All results run on a first-order
linear-elastic planar Euler-Bernoulli frame and truss solver verified to
floating-point precision against closed-form solutions and an independent solver.
The topic sits squarely within the journal's scope in computational solid and
structural mechanics.

I confirm that this manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and
is not under consideration by any other journal. As the sole author, I agree with
the contents and the stated authorship. The source code, scenarios, and scripts
that reproduce every reported number and figure are openly available under
GPL-3.0-or-later at https://github.com/fcarvajalbrown/Entropy-Collapse-Simulator.
